# Stotles

> Stotles is a B2G (business-to-government) public sector procurement and sales intelligence platform for the UK and Ireland. It aggregates procurement notices, awards, framework agreements, buyer spend, and decision-maker contacts from sources such as Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, Digital Marketplace, TED, and Public Contracts Scotland, then layers tender discovery, market intelligence, AI-assisted bid qualification, and pipeline management on top so suppliers can find and win government contracts.

Stotles is delivered as a SaaS web application (app.stotles.com). No public developer API or OpenAPI specification is published as of this writing; the platform integrates with CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot) and collaboration tools (Microsoft Teams, SharePoint, Slack, Google Drive) rather than exposing a public API surface.

## Product
- [Platform overview](https://www.stotles.com/platform): Tender discovery, market intelligence, Sales Studio, Bid Studio, and pipeline management.
- [Data coverage](https://www.stotles.com/data): 8M+ procurement notices and awards from 15K+ public buying organizations.
- [Pricing](https://www.stotles.com/pricing): Free, Basic, Growth, and Expert tiers across Sales Studio, Bid Studio, and the combined B2G Growth Engine.
